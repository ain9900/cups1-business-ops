## 3. Business fit: how we can make money with this supplier

Our market is cafés, tea shops, restaurants, bakeries, cloud kitchens, caterers, offices and event companies,
plus local wholesalers who resell to them.

| Model | How it works | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Stock plain items** | Import plain white/kraft cups, lids, bowls and boxes in bulk; sell by the carton (e.g. 1,000 pcs) | Fast delivery for customers; small businesses can buy small amounts | Needs warehouse space and upfront cash |
| **B. Custom-printed pre-orders** | Customer orders cups with their logo; we pass the factory MOQ through (e.g. 30,000 pcs) and take an advance | No stock risk; high margin per order | Long lead time (production plus sea freight is roughly 6–10 weeks) |
| **C. Hybrid (recommended)** | A + B: plain stock brings in daily customers, and custom printing gives large margins | Balanced cash flow; each part feeds the other | Two processes to run |
| D. Distributor/agent | Ask Sowinpak for exclusive or authorised distributor status in our country | Better prices and support | Needs a track record first, so ask after 2–3 orders |

**Important points for this category:**
- **Bulky, low-value goods:** cups are light but take up a lot of space. **Use sea freight (LCL first, later FCL)**.
  Air freight will destroy the margin.
- **Import duty may be high** on finished paper packaging in some countries. This decides whether the business works,
  so we must confirm the HS code (likely 4823.69 / 4823.70 for cups and plates; to be confirmed) and the full duty rate
  with a local clearing agent **before the first order**.
- **Local competition:** local cup makers may exist. Our edge would be **quality, print quality, eco/compostable options,
  wide range from one supplier, and custom design service.**

> We are an importer/distributor of food packaging in [COUNTRY], supplying cafés, restaurants and wholesalers.
> Please quote FOB [nearest port, e.g. Ningbo/Shanghai] for:
> 1. Single wall paper cups, plain white and kraft: 8 oz, 12 oz, 16 oz, with matching PP/PS lids
> 2. Double wall and ripple wall cups: 8 oz, 12 oz, with custom 1–2 colour logo
> 3. Kraft paper bowls (500/750/1000 ml) with lids, and kraft lunch/burger boxes
>
> Please include:
> - Price tiers for 30k / 50k / 100k pcs, and your lowest MOQ for **plain (unprinted)** items
> - Whether we can mix sizes/items to reach MOQ in one shipment
> - Paper GSM and PE/PLA coating, food-grade certificates (FDA/LFGB/BPI/ISO)
> - Packing details: pcs per carton, carton size, weight (to calculate container loading)
> - Production lead time and sample cost/time
> - Trade Assurance availability and payment terms (e.g. 30% deposit / 70% before shipment)
> - Possibility of becoming an authorised distributor in [COUNTRY] in future
>
> Thank you.

## 6. Risks to watch

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quality differs from sample | Medium | High | Approve a "golden sample" and arrange third-party inspection before shipment |
| High import duty kills the margin | Medium | High | Confirm duty before ordering |
| Cash stuck in large MOQ stock | Medium | High | Start with the bestsellers only; use pre-orders for custom prints |
| Shipping delays | Medium | Medium | Keep 4–6 weeks of safety stock of fast sellers |
| Supplier is new on Alibaba | Low–Medium | High | Trade Assurance, small trial, a second backup supplier |
