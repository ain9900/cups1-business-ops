## New candidates (details)

### 3. Quanzhou Dayu Paper & Plastic Products Co., Ltd.
**Store:** https://dayuco.en.alibaba.com · Quanzhou, Fujian

| Item | Finding (Alibaba, 2026-11-05) |
|---|---|
| Founded | 2009 · 51–100 staff · 9 production lines · 9,278 m² floor space |
| Products | Paper coffee cups, salad bowls, soup cups, **paper plates**, paper food boxes, heatable ramen bowls (~200 moulds) |
| Main markets | North America, Western Europe, South America, Oceania, East Asia |
| Alibaba record | 18 yrs · **5.0★ (33 reviews)** · "#6 best seller in Paper Cups" · online revenue US$1–2M · 29 top buyers |
| Performance | Reply ≤1h · on-time dispatch **100%** · reorder **36%** · avg. dispatch 32.4 days · 17 yrs exporting |
| Verification | Has a capability report ("View report for details"); inspection body not captured. Store header says "Supplier", not "Manufacturer" |

**Strengths:** strongest proven buyer satisfaction on Alibaba; only candidate that lists plates as a main line.
**Watch:** small team; confirm it is the factory itself (video call / inspection); ask for BRC, FDA, EU 10/2011 reports.
