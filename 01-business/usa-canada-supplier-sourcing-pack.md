# Summary

Source US and Canadian orders from **Sowinpak's Vietnam plant**, not its China plant. Tariffs make this the single biggest cost decision in Phase 2.

- **USA:** paper cups (HTS 4823.69.00) made in China pay about **37.5%** extra duty (25% Section 301 + 12.5% base tariff). Made in Vietnam they pay about **12.5%**.
- **Canada:** CBSA is investigating Chinese paperboard cups and containers for dumping and subsidies. Estimated margins are 75.8% + 49.6%, and a preliminary decision is due by **16 Nov 2026**. Do not quote China-made cups to Canadian clients until it is resolved.
- **Lead product:** 12oz double-wall custom-printed hot cup. The full cups, bowls and boxes range follows once the first quotes are in.
- **Primary supplier:** Zhejiang Sowinpak, which has China and Vietnam plants plus BRC, BPI and FSC certificates. **Price benchmark:** Haining Xinhua. **Small-order option:** Wenzhou Liping (MOQ 5,000).
- **Early economics:** at a placeholder $0.06 FOB, an order of 100,000 cups lands in the US at about $0.11/cup (Vietnam) to $0.12/cup (China). A 35% gross margin gives a client price of $0.17–0.19/cup, against about $0.38/cup from US online sellers. The full model is in *Cup1_Phase2_Sourcing_Model.xlsx*.

No supplier has been contacted yet. The RFQ and sample request below are ready to send.

# Supplier verification checklist

A supplier is cleared for US or Canadian client orders only when every item below is ticked.

**Legal & documents**

- ☐ Business licence matches the Alibaba company name and the factory address
- ☐ Certificates checked on the issuer's website (BRC directory, BPI product catalog, FSC info.fsc.org), not just a PDF
- ☐ FDA food-contact test report (21 CFR 176.170 for PE-coated paperboard), from an accredited lab, less than 12 months old
- ☐ PFAS-free test report (total organic fluorine), needed for states such as CA, NY, WA, MN, CO
- ☐ For a Vietnam-made order: certificate of origin and the Vietnam factory address. Confirm the cups are formed and printed there, not transshipped from China

**Capability**

- ☐ Video call or live video tour of the cup-forming and printing lines
- ☐ Printed sample matches our Pantone colours and passes a leak test (hot water, 30 min)
- ☐ Double-wall cup stays comfortable to hold with 90°C water
- ☐ Lid fits snugly with no leaks when the cup is tipped

**Commercial**

- ☐ Order placed through Alibaba Trade Assurance (payment protection)
- ☐ Third-party pre-shipment inspection agreed (e.g. SGS, QIMA, V-Trust, about $300) before the balance is paid
- ☐ Written lead time and a late-delivery penalty clause

# Target clients (US & Canada)

Start with multi-location independent café groups (3–30 stores). One order from one of them reaches the 30,000-cup MOQ, and they pay US distributors $0.13–0.38 per printed cup.

| Segment | Typical order | Where to find them | Cup1 pitch |
|---|---|---|---|
| Independent café groups (3–30 stores) | 30k–100k cups per quarter | Google Maps, Instagram, LinkedIn | Own branded cups at distributor-beating prices, with FDA and PFAS-free paperwork |
| Bubble tea & smoothie chains | 50k+ cups, cold cups too | Franchise directories, Yelp | Full branded range: cups, lids, carriers, bowls |
| Bakery-cafés & dessert / frozen-yogurt shops | 30k ice-cream cups + boxes | Instagram, local directories | Ice-cream cups (outside Canada's anti-dumping scope) + window boxes |
| Regional packaging distributors | 200k+ (full containers) | ThomasNet, trade shows (e.g. PACK EXPO) | Factory-direct private label from Vietnam, lower duty than China |
| Corporate / event catering & universities | 30k–50k | Procurement portals, LinkedIn | Compostable (BPI) branded cups for sustainability targets |

The named prospect list (20–30 businesses) is the first task of Phase 5, once sample quality is confirmed.
