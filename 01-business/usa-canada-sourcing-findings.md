# Phase 2 – Supplier Sourcing Findings (5 Nov 2026)

Target markets: USA, Canada (+ Mexico/region). Lead product: 12oz double-wall custom-printed hot cup.

## Key decisions and findings
- US duty on paper cups (HTS 4823.69.00): China origin ≈ 37.5% (25% Sec.301 + 12.5% base layer); Vietnam origin ≈ 12.5%. Re-verify before quoting; rates changed several times in 2026.
- Canada: CBSA anti-dumping + anti-subsidy case on Chinese paperboard cups and containers (PCC 2026 IN). Estimated margins 75.8% + 49.6%; preliminary decision due by 16 Nov 2026. Avoid China-origin cups for Canadian clients.
- Primary supplier: Zhejiang Sowinpak (China + Vietnam plants; BRC, BRCGS, BPI, FSC, PEFC, ISO 22000). Use the Vietnam plant for North America (to be confirmed through the RFQ).
- Backup / benchmark: Haining Xinhua (China price benchmark), Wenzhou Liping (MOQ 5,000), Dongyuan Haoyuan, Thuan Loi (Vietnam, weak profile).
- Placeholder economics (FOB $0.06, 100k cups): landed US ≈ $0.11–0.12/cup; client price at 35% GM ≈ $0.17–0.19, versus a US online benchmark ≈ $0.38 (10k qty).
- The sourcing-commission model needs a minimum fee (~$1,000 per order).
