> **FINAL BUSINESS MODEL (owner, 2026-11-06)**
>
> Cup1 (brand Cups1) is an **asset-light B2B sourcing and trading company** for custom-printed disposable food packaging. It runs two business types:
>
> | | 1. Trading company / reseller (**main**) | 2. Sourcing agent (**side**) |
> |---|---|---|
> | Revenue | **Product margin**: buy FOB, sell under the Cup1 name | **Sourcing commission**: 3–10% of FOB, minimum ≈ US$1,000/order |
> | Invoice to buyer | Cup1, full order value | Factory invoices goods; Cup1 invoices its commission only |
> | Who imports | Cup1 arranges delivery; USA & Canada sold delivered (DDP) | Buyer imports |
> | Risk | Cup1 (quality, delay, payment) | Mostly buyer; Cup1 answers for its service |
> | Customers | Café groups, chains, restaurants, caterers, procurement managers | Distributors and wholesalers |
> | Supplier visibility | Hidden | Disclosed (it is the buyer's own import) |
>
> Plus **service fees** (design, Sample Kit, QC inspection, retainers).
> **Not:** a manufacturer, a warehouse distributor, a marketplace/online shop, or a dropshipper.
> Still open (owner): target margin %, deposit %, sample policy, minimum order value, company country/bank.

## Summary
Cup1 is a **sourcing company that resells under its own name**. Buyers who don't want to deal with Alibaba
send us what they need. We source it from checked Chinese factories, manage the whole order, and sell it to the
buyer as Cup1. We earn the **margin** between our selling price and our total cost.

**Our value to the buyer:** one trusted English-speaking contact, checked factories, better prices than they could
negotiate alone, quality inspection, and shipping handled, without the buyer ever touching Alibaba.

## 2. How an order works (end to end)

| Step | What happens | Who | Money |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Requirement | Buyer submits requirement (website form / WhatsApp / email): product, size, qty, print, destination | Buyer | — |
| 2. Sourcing | We send RFQ to 2–3 shortlisted suppliers, compare price, MOQ, lead time, certificates | Cup1 | — |
| 3. Cup1 quote | We send one quote under Cup1 name: price + Incoterm + lead time + terms | Cup1 | — |
| 4. Samples (if needed) | Paid sample pack; fee credited on first order (TBD) | Buyer → Cup1 | Buyer pays sample fee |
| 5. Order confirmed | Buyer signs Proforma Invoice (PI) from Cup1 | Buyer | **Buyer pays deposit** |
| 6. Factory order | We place order with factory via **Alibaba Trade Assurance** | Cup1 | Cup1 pays factory deposit (from buyer's deposit) |
| 7. Artwork proof | Factory proof → we check → buyer approves | All | — |
| 8. Production | We follow up, send buyer progress updates/photos | Cup1 | — |
| 9. Inspection | Third-party pre-shipment inspection (e.g. SGS, Intertek, QIMA) | Cup1 | Cost included in our price |
| 10. Balance | Buyer pays balance after inspection report OK | Buyer | **Buyer pays balance** |
| 11. Factory paid & shipped | We pay factory balance; goods ship via forwarder | Cup1 | Cup1 pays factory balance |
| 12. Documents & delivery | Cup1 commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, certificates to buyer | Cup1 | — |
| 13. After-sales | Claims handling, reorder offer | Cup1 | — |

### Golden money rules
1. **Never pay a factory before the buyer has paid us at least the same amount.**
   Buyer deposit ≥ factory deposit + inspection cost.
2. **Never pay the factory balance until the buyer's balance has arrived** and the inspection has passed.
3. Pay factories **only through Alibaba Trade Assurance** (for the first orders with any supplier).
4. Release shipping documents to the buyer only after full payment (unless an L/C or agreed credit).

## 3. Pricing formula

`Cup1 price = Factory price + Inspection + Our sourcing/handling costs + Payment fees + Cup1 margin`
plus, depending on the trade term we sell on:

| Trade term | Buyer gets | We add |
|---|---|---|
| **FOB** (e.g. FOB Ningbo) | Goods loaded on ship in China; buyer arranges freight | Nothing extra |
| **CIF** (to buyer's port) | We pay sea freight + insurance to their port | Freight + insurance |
| **DDP** (to buyer's door) | We pay everything incl. import duty & taxes | Freight, insurance, duty, VAT, customs broker, delivery. **Highest risk – only after we know the country's rules** |

- **Target margin:** TBD (owner approval). **Assumption:** 10–25% depending on order size, gives room for
  negotiation and still beats most buyers' own Alibaba prices.
- Always show prices as e.g. "US$0.062/pc FOB Ningbo, 30,000 pcs".

## 3a. Revenue streams (merged 2026-11-05)

| # | Stream | How it works | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Product margin (main)** | Buy FOB from the factory, sell to the buyer as Cup1 (FOB/CIF, or delivered DDP for USA & Canada) | Cup1 carries tariff, defect and working-capital risk |
| 2 | **Sourcing commission** | Buyer imports and pays the factory; Cup1 charges a fee for research, vetting, negotiation and coordination | Market range 3–10% of order value (3–5% over $20k, 8–10% small orders) per [Epic Sourcing 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/sourcing-agent-fees-usa-2026). **Minimum ≈ US$1,000/order**. 50% up front, 50% at shipment. Best for distributors |
| 3 | **Service fees** | Design per artwork · Sample Kit (credited on first order) · pre-shipment inspection (≈ $300 pass-through + handling) · retainers for chains ($500–3,000/month market range) | Later: storage and scheduled delivery |

Rules: accept supplier-side commissions **only if disclosed** to the buyer. Protect revenue with a client service
agreement (fees, payment stages, minimum fee) and supplier non-circumvention agreements.
Recommended mix: margin model for cafés and chains; commission model with minimum fee for distributors who import themselves.

## 4. Keeping the supplier hidden (and why it matters)
If the buyer learns the factory name, they can go direct next time and we lose them.
- Neutral, unbranded cartons (or Cup1 branded cartons) – agreed with factory in writing.
- Factory never contacts the buyer; all communication through Cup1.
- Cup1 issues the commercial invoice and packing list to the buyer.
- **Watch:** the Bill of Lading "shipper" field can show the factory name. Use a freight forwarder who can
  appear as shipper, or a switch B/L. **Assumption:** to confirm with a forwarder.
- Certificates (BRCGS, FSC, test reports) can be shared with the factory name covered, but **never altered**.
- Our real protection is service: make it easier to keep buying through Cup1 than to go direct.

## 6. Main risks
| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Prevention | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality differs from sample | Medium | High | Golden sample + pre-shipment inspection | Hold factory balance; Trade Assurance claim |
| Buyer cancels after we paid factory deposit | Low–Med | Medium | Non-refundable deposit in our terms; deposit covers factory deposit | Keep deposit; resell or negotiate |
| Buyer goes direct to factory | Medium | Medium | Hide supplier (section 4); great service | Spread orders over several suppliers |
| Currency changes (CNY/USD) | Medium | Low–Med | Quote validity 15–30 days; price in USD | Re-quote |
| Shipping delays | Medium | Medium | Realistic lead times; reliable forwarder | Keep buyer updated |
| Import rules in buyer's country (food contact, plastic bans) | Medium | High | Ask destination early; get FDA/EU test reports | Offer compliant alternative material |
| Payment fraud / fake buyers | Low–Med | High | Verify company; bank transfer only for orders | Never ship before full payment |
| Sanctioned country/party | Low | Very high | Screen every buyer and destination | Refuse the order |
