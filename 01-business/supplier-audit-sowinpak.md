**Most likely explanation for the mismatch:** Sowinpak is a group. An older company, *Zhejiang Sowinpak Co., Ltd.* (Wanquan Town, Pingyang), has existed since about 2008. *Zhejiang Sowinpak Technology Co., Ltd.* is a newer company of the same group, set up in 2020 with a new factory. The website shows **group** figures (including factories in Vietnam and Indonesia); Alibaba and Intertek show **this one company**.
**Assumption:** this is our reading of the sources. Ask the supplier to confirm it in writing.

**Key rule:** sign the contract, the Proforma Invoice and the Trade Assurance order with **exactly the verified company**, *Zhejiang Sowinpak Technology Co., Ltd.*, and pay only that company's account through Alibaba.

**Worldwide compliance reminder:**
- **EU:** plastic-lined (PE) cups come under the **Single-Use Plastics Directive**. They need the "plastic in product" marking and turtle logo printed on the cup. Ask whether they can print it.
- **USA:** FDA food-contact compliance; some states (California, Washington and others) restrict PFAS in food packaging. Ask for a PFAS-free statement.
- **Middle East:** check each country (for example SASO in Saudi Arabia, ESMA in the UAE). Ask the buyer which rules apply.

> 5. Can you print the EU Single-Use Plastics marking on PE-coated cups?

> 7. Is your capacity of 2,600,000 pcs per day or per month? What lead time can you give for a 30,000 pc trial order?
> 8. Can you ship directly to our buyers in different countries (FOB Ningbo and CIF/DDP), with our own branding on the cartons (no Sowinpak name)?
