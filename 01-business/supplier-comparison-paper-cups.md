**Baseline:** Zhejiang Sowinpak Technology Co., Ltd. (see `01-business/supplier-audit-sowinpak.md`)

## Summary

Thousands of Chinese companies sell paper cups on Alibaba, but most are small workshops or traders.
I screened for: **real factory · third-party verified or stock-exchange listed · same products (cups, bowls, boxes) · export experience · food-contact certificates.**

**Best 2 to request quotes from (alongside Sowinpak):**

1. **Xiamen Jeafer Foodservice Solutions Ltd.**: 18 years exporting, SGS-verified, 15 lines, sells mostly to North America and Europe. **Best all-round match.**
2. **Hefei Hengxin Life Science and Technology Co., Ltd.**: a **stock-exchange listed** cup maker (Shenzhen 301501), about 2,600 staff, capacity 5 million cups/day, LFGB/BPI/DIN certified. **Strongest company, but slow to reply on Alibaba.**

**Backups:** Anhui Chuangxin (good for the Middle East and for plain cups), Fujian Nanwang (listed but mainly bags), Anqing Eternal (small, not verified).

## 7. Action checklist
- [ ] Send the same RFQ (from the Sowinpak audit, section 6) to **Jeafer** and **Hengxin** (via Hengxin's website email).
- [ ] Ask each for: BRCGS, FSC, FDA 21 CFR 176.170, EU 10/2011, LFGB test reports and a PFAS-free declaration.
- [ ] Ask each for MOQ for **plain** items and whether sizes can be mixed in one container.
- [ ] Ask whether they ship **direct to our buyers** in different countries with neutral (unbranded) cartons.
- [ ] Build one price table: same items (8/12/16 oz single wall, double wall, 500/750/1000 ml bowls) in US$ FOB.
- [ ] Order samples from the top 2 by price + quality.
- [ ] Verify the SGS and TÜV report numbers using the "Verify report authenticity" button on Alibaba.

## Notes for Web Developer
- The product catalogue should allow **more than one supplier per product** (main + backup) in the admin panel.

## Notes for Marketing
- Do not name suppliers on the site. Claims like "BRCGS-certified factory" may only be used for products made by a supplier that holds that certificate.
