01 / Product overview

Start with the right cup and lid.

Double-wall cups use an outer paper layer around the inner cup. For a new order, share your beverage sizes, expected quantities, and whether you need matching lids.

What to specify

  1. Target capacity in oz or mL
  2. Preferred lid or existing rim dimensions
  3. Estimated quantity per size and design
  4. Delivery postal code and requirements

02 / Artwork & printing

From brand artwork to a cup proof.

04 / Product FAQ

Before you request a quote.

Which sizes can I order?

Share your target capacity. Cups1 will confirm the sizes and lid fit available for the proposed order.

What is the minimum order?

The minimum depends on cup size, artwork, and supplier requirements. Provide your estimated volume so we can review the options.

Can I review a proof first?

Yes. Cups1 coordinates an artwork proof for approval before production.

Can I compare samples and lid fit?

Include the sizes and lid requirements you want to evaluate. Cups1 will review available samples and applicable terms.
