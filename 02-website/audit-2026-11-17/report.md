# Cups1 live website audit

**17 November 2026 · Public visitor view · cups1.com**

## Fix these first

1. **Critical — Buyers can add products but cannot check out.** The cart drawer showed one 12 oz case at $150.00. The full cart and checkout showed the WooCommerce Coming Soon screen, including on mobile checkout. If Cups1 intends to transact now, restore checkout and verify it in staging. If Cups1 is currently quote-only, remove the purchase path and offer a quote on the same selection.
2. **High — All eight product category pages in the sitemap show the same placeholder.** Publish useful category pages or redirect each to an equivalent destination; remove placeholders from the sitemap.
3. **High — Privacy and buying policies are hard to find.** The shared footer and reviewed inquiry forms lack policy links. Add the privacy notice beside data collection and make ordering, damage, and return terms easy to find before launch.
4. **Medium — Catalog filters and search generate crawlable variants without a canonical.** Keep the main storefront and intentional category landing pages distinct from search, sort, and incidental filter pages.
5. **Medium — All 42 product detail pages lack an HTML meta description and JSON-LD.** Add product descriptions and accurate structured data, keeping quote-only and purchasable products distinct.

## What worked

- The robots file and sitemap were reachable. The crawl covered 78 sitemap URLs (26 pages, 42 products, eight categories and two author archives), plus linked and test URLs, for 84 total requests across distinct URL strings. A deliberately invalid path returned HTTP 404. HTTP and www product URL variants each returned 301 to HTTPS on the apex domain. No checked, discovered clean internal HTML destination returned an unexpected 404; the separate policy-path probes were not discovered links.
- Primary catalog loaded all 42 entries. Header search opened and routed to catalog results for “paper”; partial “pap” showed six products; a nonsense term showed a clear zero-result state. Mobile menu opened and header controls appeared in Search, Cart, Menu order. No horizontal overflow on the 390 px pages sampled.
- Double-Wall 12 oz updated the catalog price and case count; the drawer and the session's cart data agreed with the selection. Increasing to two cases produced $300.00, Escape returned focus to the cart link, and removing the line returned the drawer to an empty state.
- Product quote popup displayed “Double-Wall Paper Cups · 12 oz.” Clear PLA 12 oz quote and configuration routes both prefilled the selected product and dimensions. The standalone quote form reported required fields as invalid when empty. Contact and sample forms rendered; no inquiries were submitted.
- No page exceptions or HTTP 4xx/5xx responses were captured on the 25 Chromium template and viewport visits logged in the browser sweep. This does not cover all resources behind interactions, every browser, or every visitor.

## Development and retest order

1. **Decide whether purchasing is live or quote-only.** Investigate C01 in WooCommerce first. Compare the signed-out view against staging and signed-in administrator views. See [WooCommerce Site Visibility documentation](https://woocommerce.com/document/configuring-woocommerce-settings/coming-soon-mode/).
2. **Fix storefront routes and trust content.** Address S01 and T01. Retest all affected category URLs and buyer policy links in a new Chromium session. Check that the footer and pre-submit notice agree with commercial policy.
3. **Fix crawl signals and catalog presentation.** Address S02, S03, S04 and P01. In the local [Elementor plugin](../elementor/plugin/), start with search-metadata.php, shop.php, single-product.php, public/catalog.js and public/catalog-cart.js. These paths are candidate owners; compare the deployed build and existing WooCommerce metadata before changing them.
4. **Improve wholesale content and resource weight.** Address B01, B02, F01 and H01. Verify sourcing data before publishing pack, stock, compatibility, or lead-time claims. Recheck Chromium and a real iOS/Android device.
5. **Run launch acceptance in staging.** Exercise valid and invalid quote/contact/sample requests, receipt and email, artwork upload, account login, exact-case purchasing, address handling, shipping/tax, coupons, sandbox payment and order confirmation. Do a keyboard and screen-reader pass on navigation, variation selection, forms and cart; include contrast, touch target and focus tests.

## Scope and inventory

This was a signed-out external review with a bounded crawl of sitemap and discovered same-site clean HTML links, plus targeted probes. Browser work sampled the home, catalog, product, customization, quote, sample, contact, documents, shipping, account, business, resources, logo preview, help, cart, checkout, and search templates. See the [URL inventory](url-inventory.md). Test carts were isolated to disposable browser sessions.

No forms or artwork were submitted, no order or payment was made, and no site settings were changed. Form delivery, spam controls, email confirmations, account internals, checkout options, actual Google indexing/rankings, real-user performance, privacy or policy compliance, and server/plugin security were outside verified scope. Accessibility findings are a spot check; contrast, screen reader behavior, and all keyboard paths were not exhaustively tested.
