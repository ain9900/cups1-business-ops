# Cups1 website, SEO, and marketing audit

**23 November 2026 · cups1.com · Signed-out public audit**

I checked 78 sitemap entries and 18 additional URLs, sampled the storefront and inquiry templates in Chromium, and tested a real WooCommerce variation through a temporary cart. The November 17 Coming Soon screens have cleared from checkout and all eight category pages. The current guest checkout offered no payment methods with test billing details in New York and Toronto.

## Fix these first

1. **Critical · Checkout has no payment method in the tested session.** Double-Wall Paper Cups, 12 oz, added successfully at USD $150 per case. Checkout showed “There are no payment methods available.” Verify the launch markets, gateway, shipping, and tax settings before sending buyers to checkout. [Checkout evidence](evidence/checkout.png)
2. **High · Privacy, terms, and returns are missing from the buyer journey.** The usual policy paths return 404, the reviewed forms lack a privacy notice link, and checkout displays agreement text without a usable path to the policies. Publish approved policies, link them at collection and checkout, and configure consent before turning on optional tracking.
3. **High · Conversion measurement is unverified.** The public templates provided no positive evidence of Meta Pixel, GA4, GTM, or a consent manager. Confirm what is installed and instrument successful quote submissions first. The custom cart and two inquiry flows need explicit event tests. [Marketing implementation plan](marketing-implementation.md)
4. **High · Confirm indexing in Search Console.** The saved screenshot shows the sitemap index last read November 19 with zero discovered pages, and another shows a homepage indexing request. The public index and all four child sitemaps are currently reachable and list 78 URLs. The live Google indexing decision still needs account review. [Saved sitemap screenshot](../../search-console-sitemap.jpg) · [Saved URL inspection screenshot](../../search-console-indexing.jpg) · [Live sitemap](https://cups1.com/wp-sitemap.xml)
5. **High · Product and category SEO needs a consistent template.** All 42 product pages lack a meta description and JSON-LD. The main catalog and eight category pages lack a canonical and meta description, while product search and filters expose competing URLs. Fix the core landing pages and validate catalog data before exporting shopping feeds.

## What passed and what remains

- HTTP and www variants of the catalog converge on the HTTPS apex. The WordPress sitemap and four child files respond. The eight category pages now render distinct catalog headings. The 42 product pages have one base canonical each. [URL inventory](url-inventory.md)
- The catalog showed 42 entries at desktop and mobile widths. The “paper” search returned six entries and an intentionally unmatched query returned zero. Mobile header controls appeared in Search, Cart, Menu order, and the menu opened. The product detail displayed size buttons and hid the separate case-pack selector. The quote popup carried the selected Double-Wall 12 oz context and displayed a work email field and contact consent wording.
- Checkout, inquiry delivery, uploads, CRM, spam handling, and gateway/order emails need controlled testing. No inquiry, payment, or order was submitted. Private settings, plugin inventory, server-side tracking, existing catalog accounts, actual Google rankings, and field performance require account or hosting access.
- A browser-verification page appeared on automated fresh visits. Verify normal browsers and Google Live URL Inspection before drawing conclusions about crawl effects. Browser measurements taken on verification pages were excluded.

## Rollout order

1. Verify payments and shipping, publish buyer policies, and confirm inquiry delivery. Test a complete US and Canadian order in staging and one accepted lead per inquiry type.
2. Fix indexing, metadata, taxonomy pages, and sitemap quality. Recheck Google-selected canonical and indexing for priority URLs after deployment.
3. Add consent, GTM/GA4 and Meta Pixel/CAPI. Validate primary B2B lead events first, then the case sales funnel when checkout is operational.
4. Generate a CTX Feed review export of approved sellable variants. Match item IDs to Meta events and verify case price, availability, landing links, and destination diagnostics before activating catalog campaigns. [Marketing implementation plan](marketing-implementation.md)
