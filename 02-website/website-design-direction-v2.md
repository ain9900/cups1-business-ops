## 1. Summary – three things that change the plan
1. **Navy + orange is already taken.** Leeline Sourcing (a China sourcing agent, like us) uses navy #141F33 + orange #FF7701, almost the same as Cups1 #00213C + #FE7702. LogoCup (#333369 + #FF6F1A) and KimEcopak (#021D4B + #FFAC26) are close too. Cups1 can't stand out on colour alone. The teal/kraft conclusion in the v1 teardown is now out of date.
2. **The closest-model rivals sell a service, not a shop.** PakFactory and the sourcing agents lead with a quote, a dedicated expert and an end-to-end promise. Cart sites (noissue, LogoCup) are a weaker template for a 30,000+ cup reseller.
3. **Nobody shows the paperwork.** None of the 18 sites shows a test report or a delivered-price breakdown before quoting. That gap fits "Fully documented" and should be the site's visual signature.

## 3. Colour rule (recommendation)
- **Orange = action only:** the main button ("Get a delivered quote") and price highlights. Never headings or large backgrounds.
- **Navy = authority:** headings, top bar, footer, outline buttons.
- **Paper #F7F3EC as the page background** (not white). None of the navy-orange competitors does this.
- **Verified green #2E7D4F only beside real documents.** Keep the logo leaf green #67A42D out of the website UI: it is close to Sowinpak's #6AA534 and implies an eco claim.

## 5. Recommended homepage (about 6,000–7,000 px)
1. Top bar: delivered to USA & Canada, duties included, WhatsApp
2. Header: Products · How it works · Documents · For distributors · **Get a quote**
3. Hero: client logo on a real 12oz cup; "Your brand. Every cup. Fully documented."; fact row (minimum · weeks · papers); buttons **Get a delivered quote** (orange) + **Request a Sample Kit** (navy outline)
4. Choose your path (3 buyer types)
5. Product range (6) with minimum, lead time, "from" delivered price
6. Alibaba-yourself vs Cups1 comparison
7. How it works: quote → proof → production → inspection → delivered
8. See the paperwork (document cards)
9. Sample Kit offer
10. "Something else? We source it." short form
11. FAQ (minimums, duties, artwork, lead times, payment)
12. Footer: contact + hours, policies, cookie settings

Type: Inter 800 headlines, 400/600 body, no all-caps text blocks. Shape: 8px corners; pills only for status chips. Photos: real Cups1 samples on paper/kraft; no factory or trade-show photos (supplier hidden). Stats, reviews and logos stay out until real (claims rule).

## 6. Do / don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Minimum, lead time and delivered price on every card | Orange headings or large orange areas |
| Delivered (DDP) price explained: product + freight + duty | Factory or trade-show photos |
| One named, real contact | "#1", client counts or ratings we don't have |
| Real documents, redacted where needed | "Eco"/"recyclable" without a document |
| 5–6 field forms + "How did you hear about us?" | Discount pop-ups / "10% OFF" bars |
| Cookie banner before ad pixels | Online designer or cart (quotes fit 30k+ orders) |
