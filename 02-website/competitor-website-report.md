**Prepared by:** Web Developer + Marketing Expert · **Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** Research complete, for owner review

**Method (Fact):** each homepage loaded live; fonts, colours, buttons and page length are computed values. Optional cookies declined. Mr. Takeout Bags not measured (bot protection); scored from its product page text. Customer counts, savings and rankings are the companies' own claims.

## 1. Six findings
1. **Nobody shows proof of compliance.** 4 of 14 homepages mention a certificate; none shows a test report, PFAS declaration or delivered-price breakdown. This is our open space ("Fully documented").
2. **The closest rivals look small and generic.** Kings Cups, Custom Disposables, Direct Source Procurement: standard themes, stock/AI-looking photos, no real client proof.
3. **The quote is the product.** 12 of 15 lead with a quote/contact action. Kings Cups promises an "all-inclusive quote" (price, shipping, taxes).
4. **Numbers are rare, and they win when shown.** Only CupPrint (1,000 pcs / 15 days), Custom Disposables (10 cases / 6–8 weeks) and noissue (cards) show minimum and lead time up front. JingSourcing publishes factory prices: custom PE paper cups 8/12/16oz $0.04–0.06/pc, lids $0.01–0.02 (price anchor for our commission business).
5. **The best new ideas are about repeat business.** Earth Brands: yearly price-locked contracts, management portal, multi-site ordering. noissue: Reorder / Track order / My Quotes in the header.
6. **Visual proof beats claims.** The strongest heroes show a real branded product (CupPrint, SupplyCaddy, Earth Brands). Earth Brands has a plain-vs-branded drag slider.

## 6. Blueprint changes
- **Homepage:** top bar (delivered, duties incl., WhatsApp) → header with Documents + Track order + Get a quote → hero (real cup + fact line + 2 buttons) → 4 service icons → 3 buyer paths → products with minimum/lead time → (Phase 2 slider) → 3 steps + delivered-price example → document cards → Sample Kit, short quote form, FAQ, footer.
- **Product page:** add print options (Pantone/CMYK), artwork file rules and over/under-run tolerance (8–10%) tab.
- **Quote form:** product+size, quantity (30k/50k/100k/other), delivery city, logo upload or "send later", name + work email, "How did you hear about us?"; reply promise (2 business days, to confirm).

## 7. Do / don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Minimum + lead time in first screen and on cards | "#1", "Top 1", unverifiable "30% cheaper" |
| Real branded cup as hero | Stacked bars, quizzes, pop-ups (KimEcopak) |
| Homepage ~5,500–7,000 px | Company name as headline (Budget Branders) |
| Fast-loading images (Green Paper's blank tiles) | Factory/trade-show photos (supplier hidden) |
| Cookie consent with "Essential only" (Karat) | Slider heroes |
| State over/under-run and artwork rules | "Sustainable"/"eco" without a document |
