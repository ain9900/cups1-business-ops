**Prepared by:** Marketing Expert (with Business Expert) · **Date:** 2026-11-06 · **Status:** Research complete, for owner review

**Labels:** **Fact** = stated on the company's own site or in a cited article (company claims marked "claims"). **Estimate** = our judgement (threat level).

## How the 15 were chosen
1. **Same model (A):** sources overseas or through partner factories, sells under its own name to food businesses, quote-based. These are our closest rivals.
2. **Same buyer, different model (B):** distributors and brands with a custom-print program that café groups already use.
3. **Same service (C):** sourcing agents competing for our commission business with distributors.
Manufacturers selling direct (Sowinpak, YOON, KayRun, CupsPak) are listed separately under "also watch": they are suppliers more than competitors.

## Also watch
- **Small-run online brands** (compete for the smallest cafés): YourBrandCafe, LogoCup, Templi (custom-print marketplace; resells e.g. Stanpac cups), PakFactory, Arka.
- **Factories selling direct** (also potential suppliers): Sowinpak (our primary), YOON, KayRun (Dongguan; 50,000–100,000 custom MOQ), **CupsPak** (Jiangsu, cupspak.com).
- ⚠️ **Name risk:** "CupsPak" is a Chinese paper-cup maker selling worldwide. Its name is close to "Cups1". Add it to the trademark search.

## What this means for Cups1
1. **The exact model exists but is small and fragmented.** Kings Cups, Custom Disposables, Budget Branders and Direct Source Procurement all say "we source it for you". None shows compliance documents or explains a delivered price. Our "Fully documented" position is still open.
2. **Two price/speed squeezes:** US-made, low-minimum players (Earth Brands with new money, CupPrint) win speed; factories and noissue win the price anchor. Cups1 must win on **30k–100k cups, delivered, documented, one contact**.
3. **Canada is contested by KimEcopak** (local warehouses, 10,000 MOQ). Our edge there: Vietnam origin while the CBSA case on Chinese cups is open. Re-check after 2026-11-16.
4. **Chains are being won by procurement specialists** (SupplyCaddy). Start with café groups of 3–30 stores and grow into chains.
5. **Commission business:** generalist agents (Jing, Leeline) are cheap. Our pitch to distributors is packaging specialism + compliance files.
