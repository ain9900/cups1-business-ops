## Summary

I visited 6 websites that sell the same products to the same kind of buyers (cafés, restaurants, caterers, wholesalers). They fall into 4 models:

| # | Company | Country | Model | Closest to Cup1? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **CupPrint** | Ireland (Huhtamaki group) | Custom-printed cups, quote-based, ships to 47 countries | ⭐⭐⭐ **Very close** |
| 2 | **Packhelp** | Poland / EU | Custom + plain packaging, online designer, instant prices | ⭐⭐⭐ Very close |
| 3 | **Hotpack** | UAE / Middle East | Big manufacturer; corporate site + separate online shop | ⭐⭐ (Middle East model) |
| 4 | **Vegware** | UK | Own eco brand; shop + distributors + custom print | ⭐⭐ (eco brand model) |
| 5 | **Restaurantware** | USA | Own-brand foodservice supplies, 20,000+ products, e-shop | ⭐ (large catalogue) |
| 6 | **WebstaurantStore** | USA | Huge B2B marketplace, price per case | ⭐ (price benchmark) |

**Three biggest lessons for Cup1:**
1. **Two buying paths on every product:** "Buy plain stock" (price per carton, add to cart) and "Custom print" (configure → get a quote). Every winner does this.
2. **Show the numbers early:** MOQ, price per piece, lead time and countries served sit in the first screen. Buyers decide in seconds.
3. **Trust is the product:** stats (years, customers, countries), client logos, named testimonials, certificates and a real address are on every homepage.

## 1. CupPrint (cupprint.com): the model to copy
**Who:** Irish company founded 2009, part of Huhtamaki. Custom-printed paper cups for cafés up to global chains.

| Area | What they do |
|---|---|
| Hero | **"Custom printed paper cups: Your brand, front and center."** Sub-line packs every key fact: *made in the EU, from 1000 pcs, delivery in 15 days, recyclable & compostable* |
| Main buttons | **GET A QUOTE** (repeated everywhere) and CONTACT US |
| Stats bar | 16 Years · 20k Customers · 300B Cups · 47 Countries · 3 Locations |
| Menu | Products (by cup type) · Design Services · Design Templates · Sustainability · About Us · Blog · Contact |
| Product page | Step-by-step choices: **Size** (8/12/16/20 oz with "best for…" hints) → **Material** (Regular PE / Low-plastic recyclable / Compostable) → **Finish** (Gloss/Matt) → Special finish → **Quantity** (1,000 to 100,000+) → **ADD TO QUOTE** |
| Smart idea | Product page asks **"I want to serve cups on my premises"** vs **"I want to resell cups"**, splitting end users from wholesalers |
| Design help | Free in-house design service + downloadable **design templates** |
| Trust | Long named testimonials (Applegreen, Zeus), "Trusted By" logo strip, Huhtamaki parent |

**Copy:** quote basket ("Add to quote" instead of cart), size guide text, end-user vs reseller split, stats bar.
**Weakness:** no prices shown at all, so buyers must wait for a quote.

## 2. Packhelp (packhelp.co.uk): best online buying experience
**Who:** EU packaging platform for small and mid businesses. Cups are one category among boxes, bags, labels.

| Area | What they do |
|---|---|
| Category page | Every product card shows **"Min. 50 / 100 / 1000 pieces"**; badges like **ECO CHOICE 🌱**; filters for Customisation, Industry, Sustainability |
| Product page | **Instant tier prices**: 1000 pcs £0.21/pc → 2000 £0.19 → 3000 £0.17 → 4000 £0.15 · options for size, PE or BIO coating, single/double wall · **delivery date shown** ("10 December") · total price per 1000 |
| Design | 3 ways: **Customise design** (online 3D editor) · **Design on a dieline (AI, PDF)** · **Add to cart and design later** · plus "Talk to our experts" |
| Extras | **Sample packs**, related products (lids, cup holders), cashback wallet, promo codes, 15% newsletter discount |

**Copy:** price-tier table, MOQ on every card, delivery-date estimate, sample packs, "design later" option.
**Weakness:** heavy discount banners feel retail rather than B2B; cups are lost among many other product types.

## 3. Hotpack (hotpackglobal.com): Middle East giant
**Who:** UAE manufacturer since 1995, 25 factories (UAE, KSA, Qatar, Malaysia, India, UK, Jordan), 25,000+ customers, 4,000+ products.

| Area | What they do |
|---|---|
| Top bar | Phone, email, **Download Catalogue**, **language** (English/Arabic), **Select Country** |
| Menu | About Us · Products · Manufacturing · Sustainability · Resources · Get in touch · **Shop Online** (separate shop) |
| Homepage | Stats (25+ factories, 25,000+ customers, 4,000+ products, "#1 in Middle East") → corporate video → **"Serving industries"** list (Café/Catering, Hotels, Bakeries, Cloud Kitchens, Govt, Healthcare, E-commerce…) → client logos → factory map |
| FAQ | Has a page answering "Do you serve small businesses and startups?" |

**Copy:** country selector + Arabic, catalogue PDF download, **shop by industry**, FAQ for small buyers.
**Weakness:** corporate site and shop are split, so buyers jump between two sites.

## 4. Vegware (vegware.com): eco brand model
**Who:** UK, B Corp, plant-based compostable packaging, 35k+ customers.

| Area | What they do |
|---|---|
| Hero | **"Packaging made from plants"** · buttons: SHOP NOW / Customise your packaging |
| Menu | products · about · news · custom print · FAQ · contact · login/**Register** |
| Homepage | Impact ticker (B Corp, composting sites, products launched) → bestsellers by category (Hot cups, Cold cups, Takeaway, Containers) → testimonial → custom print → **"Keen to compost?"** lead form |
| Footer | Track your order · **Reorder** · Delivery & returns · Samples · **Catalogue PDF** · Case studies · Brand support · policies (quality, environmental) |

**Copy:** Reorder link, Samples, Catalogue PDF, case studies, environmental/quality policy pages.
**Weakness:** UK-focused; we are worldwide.

## 5. Restaurantware (restaurantware.com): big catalogue brand
**Who:** US own-brand foodservice supplier, 20,000+ products.

- Menu by **department** (Disposables, Take-out, Tabletop…) plus **"Customizable Products"** and **"Business Type"**.
- Prices shown **per box** (e.g. "$35.04 / 100 count box") with item codes.
- **Buying guides** in the menu ("Coffee cups buying guide").
- Loyalty: **RW Rewards** points, free shipping sign-up, "We plant a tree for every order" (430,259 trees).
- Product lines grouped into named **collections** (Bio Tek, Coppetta…).

**Copy:** buying guides, shop by business type, item codes, loyalty programme later.

## 8. Positioning gap for Cup1

Most competitors serve **one region** (EU, UK, US or Middle East) and ship from local stock. CupPrint and Packhelp have **low MOQs (1,000 pcs) but European prices** (~£0.15–0.21/pc printed).

**Cup1 opportunity (Assumption, to test):** *"Factory-direct custom cups and food packaging, shipped worldwide: lower price at container quantities, with English-speaking support, checked factories and quality inspection."*
Target buyers who need **10,000–100,000+ pieces**: café chains, distributors/wholesalers, food brands, event caterers in regions with fewer local makers (Middle East, Africa, South/Southeast Asia, Oceania).

Proposed hero (draft):
> **Custom paper cups & food packaging, factory-direct, shipped worldwide.**
> From 10,000 pcs · BRCGS & FSC-certified factories · Free design · Samples in 7 days · FOB / CIF / DDP

**Assumption:** MOQ, sample time and certificates must match the final supplier deal before we publish them.
