# Competitor pricing, price logic & delivery

**Audit date:** 2026-11-11 · **Status:** Partial. Live KimEcopak variants and selected first-party product and program pages reviewed. [Claude's original report](https://claude.ai/artifact/4WFDctYzxAgRPaGv8hxjnN)

## Corrections to the original report

- Kings Cups publishes 5,000-piece packages for both wall types, including shipping and taxes. Its [homepage](https://kingscups.com/) and [FAQ](https://kingscups.com/pages/frequently-asked-questions) give different arrival estimates (about six and eight weeks).
- Good Start's specific 12 oz paper hot cup requires 50 cases of 1,000 and shows 14–16 weeks from proof approval. The 10,000 starting minimum applies to some products across its range, including a separate 12 oz PLA cold cup. The prices below are the displayed suggested case prices. The configured checkout totals remain unverified.
- Custom Disposables shows an in-stock ripple-wall case price. The listing does not establish that custom printing is included.
- KimEcopak's selector lists 10,000, but the SKU title and specifications show different minimums. The SKU advertises doorstep delivery while its [general shipping policy](https://www.kimecopak.ca/pages/shipping-policy) assigns applicable duties and taxes to the buyer. Confirm the minimum and duties in a written quote. Use the displayed figures as CAD list prices. The 100,000 and 200,000 tiers include full color, so the 100,000 increase per cup should not be interpreted as a same-specification volume increase.
- CupPrint USA advertises readiness to ship in 15 days. Transit and arrival depend on the order. The [quick quote page](https://cupprint.com/us/get-a-paper-cup-quote/) displays a region selector. Its visible form asks for name, email, phone, country, product, size, quantity, buyer type, consent and optional brief/artwork. Its quantity options include 10k and 50k but omit 30k. The page identifies the visible form as European and links to a separate North America route.
