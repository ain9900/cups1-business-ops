# Cup1

Cup1's visual identity: a factory-direct packaging partner that puts a café's brand on every cup, with the compliance paperwork done. The look is **clean, practical and proof-led**, like well-made packaging and a tidy spec sheet. It is not a trendy eco brand.

This is a first system built from the Phase 3 brand strategy, not from an existing brand. The logo is a working mark; have a designer convert its text to outlines and refine it before printing it on physical goods.

## Content fundamentals

- **Voice:** clear, practical, quietly confident. Lead with the specific fact: size, print, price, date.
  - Yes: "12oz double-wall, 2-color print, delivered to Toronto for $0.17/cup."
  - No: "Amazing premium eco solutions for your brand journey!"
- **Tagline:** *Your brand. Every cup. Fully documented.*
- **Claims:** if we can't attach the document, we don't make the claim. No unqualified "eco-friendly", "green" or "recyclable". "Compostable" only for third-party (e.g. BPI) certified items, qualified as commercially compostable. No reviews or client logos until they are real.
- **Spelling:** American English. Prices are in USD unless the client asks for CAD.

## Visual foundations

**Colour.** The ground is `surface-100` (uncoated paper) with `ink` text. `brand` (Harbor Teal) carries the logo, primary buttons and key headings. `kraft` is a decorative accent that recalls kraft cups and the sample-kit box; it is never used for text (use `kraft-text` instead). `verified` green is reserved for compliance proof ("Test report on file", certificate badges), so it keeps its meaning. `error` is for form errors only. Brand-to-kraft-to-ink is the signature trio.

**Type.** One family, Inter, loaded from Google Fonts. Headlines (`display`, `h1`, `h2`) use the `display` family at ExtraBold (800) with tight letter-spacing (-0.02em), heavy and sturdy like printed packaging. Everything else uses the `sans` family at 400/600. Use `eyebrow` in uppercase with 0.08em letter-spacing, in `kraft-text`, above section titles. Use `price` with tabular numbers in pricing tables.

**Layout.** Generous white space; a 12-column grid with `space-6` gutters; sections separated by `space-12`. Cards use `surface-200`, `radius-md` and a `line` border, with no shadows.

**Shape.** Two shapes come from the logo: the **tapered cup** and the **rim pill** (`radius-pill`). Use the rim pill for status chips and decorative bands. Corners are otherwise `radius-md`.

**Imagery.** Real product photos from Cup1 samples, on `surface-0` or a kraft backdrop, with soft daylight. Never stock photos passed off as our product. Avoid eco clichés: leaves, globes, hands holding seedlings.

## Iconography

Simple 1.5px line icons with rounded caps (a Lucide-style set), at 20px or 24px, in `ink` or `brand`. Use a check-shield icon in `verified` only beside a real compliance document.

## Logos

See the Logos asset group: the mark (a cup with a "1" knocked out), the horizontal logo, and a reversed version for `brand` or dark grounds. Clear space around the logo is the height of the rim pill (1/8 of the mark height) on all sides. The minimum size is 24px for the mark and 96px wide for the horizontal logo. Never recolour the logo, stretch it, or add effects to it.
