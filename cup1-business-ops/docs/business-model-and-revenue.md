# Cup1 Business Model & Revenue (as of 5 Nov 2026)

## Business model

Cup1 is a **B2B sourcing and branded-packaging company**. It helps businesses get products made in Asian factories with their own brand on them, without the work of importing.

| Element | Current decision |
|---|---|
| What we sell | Custom-printed food-service packaging: cups, lids, bowls, takeaway boxes with the client's logo. Lead SKU: 12oz double-wall hot cup |
| Who we sell to | Café groups (3–30 stores), bubble-tea and dessert chains, regional distributors in the **USA & Canada** |
| Order sweet spot | 30,000–100,000 cups per order |
| Where it's made | Certified Asian factories via Alibaba. Primary: Zhejiang Sowinpak, using its **Vietnam** plant for North America |
| What we do | Supplier vetting, price negotiation, artwork and proofs, QC inspection, shipping and customs, compliance paperwork (FDA, PFAS-free) |
| Positioning | "Your brand. Every cup. Fully documented." Factory-direct, delivered price, compliance included |
| Asset-light | No factory, no warehouse; value lies in service, so we must protect against clients going straight to the factory |

## Revenue streams

### 1. Product margin (main)

Buy FOB from the factory and sell delivered (DDP) to the client. Example with a placeholder FOB of $0.06: 100,000 cups, Vietnam → USA.

| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Client pays Cup1 (revenue, ≈ $0.169/cup) | $16,876 |
| Product + printing plates | −$6,300 |
| Freight, insurance, duties, customs fees, delivery | −$4,669 |
| **Gross profit** | **$5,906** |
| Cup1 order costs (samples, QC, bank fees) | −$700 |
| **Net profit (before overheads and tax)** | **≈ $5,206 (≈ 31%)** |

- Payment flow: client 50% deposit / 50% before shipment. Factory usually 30% / 70%.
- Risks: working capital needed; Cup1 carries tariff and defect risk.

### 2. Sourcing commission

The client imports and pays the factory; Cup1 charges a service fee.

- Market range: **3–10% of order value** (3–5% for orders over $20k; 8–10% for small orders), per [Epic Sourcing 2026](https://www.epicsourcing.co/post/sourcing-agent-fees-usa-2026).
- Use a **minimum fee of ≈ $1,000 per order**. 8% of a $6,000 order is only $480; after $700 of costs, the minimum fee leaves about $300 net.
- Collect 50% up front and 50% at shipment.

### 3. Service fees

Design per artwork · Sample Kit (credited against the first order) · pre-shipment inspection (≈ $300 pass-through + handling) · monthly retainer for chains ($500–3,000/month market range) · later: storage and scheduled delivery.

## Rules

- Accept supplier-side commissions **only if disclosed** to the client.
- Protect revenue with a client service agreement (fees, payment stages, minimum fee) and supplier non-circumvention agreements.
- Recommended mix: margin model for cafés and chains; commission model with a minimum fee for distributors who import themselves.
- **Price risk:** noissue lists custom PE cups "from $0.10" (MOQ 1,000, 3 weeks). Confirm real supplier quotes before publishing any price.
- Receiving USD in Bangladesh: confirm the process with an accountant under Bangladesh Bank rules. (Not legal or financial advice.)
