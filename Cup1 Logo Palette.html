Our recommendation

  1. Keep the v1.2 idea (“Cup” + a cup holding the 1). It is the only structure that stays readable in the navbar and as a 16px favicon.
  2. Font: F2 Nunito Black or F3 Bricolage Grotesque if you want the name warmer than today's Inter. Both keep the B2B tone.
  3. Colour: stay teal (v1.2) or move to S1 navy + orange if you prefer your samples' colours. Avoid the green directions unless we can document eco claims.
  4. Taglines and illustrations from your samples work on packaging, banners and decks (S5), never in the navbar or favicon.
